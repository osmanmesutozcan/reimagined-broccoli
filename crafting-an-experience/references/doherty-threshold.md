# Doherty Threshold

## Core idea

Quick system responses help maintain a productive rhythm between a person's action and the next decision.

Source: [Doherty Threshold — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/doherty-threshold/).

## Apply in context

Acknowledge actions promptly and make longer operations understandable. For example, show that an upload has started, expose its real state, and preserve a recovery path.

## Use judgment

The site's 400 ms figure is a historical guide, not a universal performance guarantee. Acknowledgment differs from completion. Use measured progress when available and an honest indeterminate state otherwise.

## Check the experience

Measure action-to-feedback and action-to-result separately under realistic conditions. Watch for repeated clicks, uncertainty, or abandonment.
