# Law of Prägnanz

## Core idea

People tend to resolve complex or ambiguous visual patterns into simpler, coherent forms.

Source: [Law of Prägnanz — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/law-of-pr%C3%A4gnanz/).

## Apply in context

Make the intended visual structure easy to perceive. For example, simplify a status symbol's silhouette and pair it with a label when its meaning is consequential.

## Use judgment

Perceptual simplicity does not imply that every workflow should lose features. An elegant shape can still be unfamiliar or ambiguous.

## Check the experience

Show the visual briefly and ask what it represents. Check whether simplification preserves the distinctions the task requires.
