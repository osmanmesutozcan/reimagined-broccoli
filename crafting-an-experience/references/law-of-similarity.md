# Law of Similarity

## Core idea

Shared visual properties suggest a common group or role, even across distance.

Source: [Law of Similarity — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/law-of-similarity/).

## Apply in context

Give matching roles a consistent appearance. For example, make interactive links recognizable throughout a settings page and distinguish them from informational labels.

## Use judgment

Identical styling can falsely imply identical behavior. Color alone is insufficient for communicating a distinction to everyone.

## Check the experience

Ask what is interactive and which items behave alike. Check that labels, shape, or other cues retain meaning when color is unavailable.
