# Fitts’s Law

## Core idea

Acquiring a pointing target generally takes more effort as distance increases or the target becomes smaller.

Source: [Fitts’s Law — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/fittss-law/).

## Apply in context

Consider the actual hit area, spacing, and likely pointer or finger position. For example, make a row action comfortably selectable near the item it affects.

## Use judgment

Bigger controls can crowd nearby choices; proximity can make accidental destructive actions easier. Pointing geometry alone does not establish keyboard or assistive-technology usability.

## Check the experience

Try representative devices and input methods. Observe missed targets, accidental neighbors, and whether the control is reachable without awkward movement.
