# Peak-End Rule

## Core idea

Intense moments and the ending can disproportionately shape how an experience is remembered.

Source: [Peak-End Rule — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/peak-end-rule/).

## Apply in context

Identify anxiety, relief, value, and closure in the journey. For example, after a submission, show what was received, what happens next, and how to return.

## Use judgment

A pleasant ending does not repair a failed task. Address severe negative moments as well as the finish, and match the emotional tone to the situation.

## Check the experience

Ask what someone remembers after the task and whether they know its outcome. Compare this account with difficulties observed along the way.
