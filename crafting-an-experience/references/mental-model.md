# Mental Model

## Core idea

People use an internal explanation of a system to predict how it works; that explanation may differ from implementation.

Source: [Mental Model — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/mental-model/).

## Apply in context

Discover users' vocabulary and expectations through concrete predictions. For example, clarify whether archiving hides an item, deletes it, or pauses its effects.

## Use judgment

The team's model and a competitor's interface are clues, not proof of the user's model. When expectations cannot match reality, explain the difference at the decision point.

## Check the experience

Ask what an action will change, then what changed afterward. Look for mismatches about persistence, scope, or reversibility.
