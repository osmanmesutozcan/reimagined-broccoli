# Pareto Principle

## Core idea

A relatively small share of causes may account for a large share of outcomes.

Source: [Pareto Principle — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/pareto-principle/).

## Apply in context

Use observed task frequency and friction to find high-impact improvements. For example, prioritize a repeated import failure that consumes a large share of support effort.

## Use judgment

The 80/20 ratio is illustrative, not a prediction about this product. Frequency alone can overlook rare severe failures or exclude smaller user groups.

## Check the experience

Compare the expected benefit with usage and severity evidence. After a change, check whether the targeted burden actually fell.
