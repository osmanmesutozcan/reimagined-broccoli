# Cognitive Load

## Core idea

A task consumes mental effort through its inherent demands and through the way the interface presents it.

Source: [Cognitive Load — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/cognitive-load/).

## Apply in context

Identify what someone must interpret, remember, or calculate. Remove effort unrelated to their goal. For example, show a calculated order total beside editable quantities instead of requiring mental arithmetic.

## Use judgment

A sparse screen can still require difficult inference. Keep information that helps someone understand consequential choices, even when it makes the screen fuller.

## Check the experience

Walk through a realistic task and identify pauses for interpretation or calculation. Does the change reduce those burdens without introducing hidden steps?
