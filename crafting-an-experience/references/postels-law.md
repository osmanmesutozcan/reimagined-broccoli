# Postel’s Law

## Core idea

In its UX application, accommodate understandable variation in input while producing clear, dependable results.

Source: [Postel’s Law — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/postels-law/).

## Apply in context

Accept harmless format differences and explain genuine boundaries. For example, accept spaces in a phone number and show the interpreted value when needed.

## Use judgment

Tolerance requires clear meaning. Ambiguous dates or consequential values need clarification; preserve validation and data integrity. This UX analogy is not blanket advice for protocol parsing.

## Check the experience

Try realistic variants, invalid values, and ambiguous input. Can people see what was accepted and correct a problem without losing their work?
