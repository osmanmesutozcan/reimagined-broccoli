# Working Memory

## Core idea

People temporarily hold and manipulate information to complete tasks, and this capacity is limited.

Source: [Working Memory — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/working-memory/).

## Apply in context

Keep needed information available at the point of use. For example, compare shortlisted products together and carry earlier selections into a checkout summary.

## Use judgment

A fixed capacity or duration is an unreliable design budget. Familiarity, distraction, and the task change the burden; externalizing everything can also create clutter.

## Check the experience

Interrupt a realistic task, then resume it. Can someone recover their place and make the next decision without reconstructing prior information?
