# Jakob’s Law

## Core idea

People carry expectations from familiar products into a new experience.

Source: [Jakob’s Law — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/jakobs-law/).

## Apply in context

Identify conventions this audience actually knows and preserve their meaning. For example, use established selection and save behavior so users can focus on their work.

## Use judgment

A familiar pattern may fit poorly or carry existing flaws. A deliberate departure can help when its benefit outweighs learning cost and feedback makes the new behavior understandable.

## Check the experience

Before an action, ask what someone expects to happen. Compare their prediction with the result, including repeat use after learning.
