# Flow

## Core idea

Focused involvement is supported when challenge matches skill and people have clear goals, feedback, and a sense of control.

Source: [Flow — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/flow/).

## Apply in context

Preserve context during sustained work. For example, an editor can offer direct feedback and discoverable shortcuts while keeping guidance available to new users.

## Use judgment

Efficiency for an expert can overwhelm a beginner. Design for the task's appropriate rhythm and allow stopping; sustained engagement is useful only while serving the person's goal.

## Check the experience

Observe a complete work session. Where do people lose their place, wait unnecessarily, or need help beyond their current skill?
