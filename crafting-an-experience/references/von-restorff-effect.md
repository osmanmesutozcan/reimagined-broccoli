# Von Restorff Effect

## Core idea

An item that differs from its surroundings is more likely to be remembered.

Source: [Von Restorff Effect — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/von-restorff-effect/).

## Apply in context

Reserve distinction for information that deserves it. For example, make the current selection recognizable through a label and shape as well as color.

## Use judgment

Multiple competing highlights weaken relative distinction. Memorability does not guarantee understanding; motion and color choices also affect accessibility.

## Check the experience

Ask what stood out and what it meant. Check whether the intended priority remains clear without color or animation.
