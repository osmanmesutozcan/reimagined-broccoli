# Law of Common Region

## Core idea

A shared boundary or background makes elements appear to belong together.

Source: [Law of Common Region — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/law-of-common-region/).

## Apply in context

Use containers to clarify scope. For example, place a delivery method, its price, and its conditions inside one region so the relationship is visible.

## Use judgment

Excessive nested containers compete for attention. A boundary can imply a relationship that the behavior does not support.

## Check the experience

Ask which information and actions belong to a group. Check that this interpretation survives narrow layouts and content of different lengths.
