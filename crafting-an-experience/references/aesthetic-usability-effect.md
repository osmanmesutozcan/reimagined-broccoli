# Aesthetic-Usability Effect

## Core idea

Polish can make an experience feel easier and increase tolerance for small difficulties. Perceived ease and successful use are different outcomes.

Source: [Aesthetic-Usability Effect — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/aesthetic-usability-effect/).

## Apply in context

Use visual coherence to support confidence: readable type, deliberate spacing, and stable hierarchy. For example, refine a booking summary so its price, dates, and action feel orderly and dependable.

## Use judgment

A favorable impression can conceal task failures during feedback. Keep visual preference separate from observed usability; decoration that weakens legibility defeats the purpose.

## Check the experience

Have someone complete the booking, then ask about ease. Compare their impression with mistakes and hesitation.
