# Miller’s Law

## Core idea

Immediate memory is limited, and meaningful grouping influences how much people can retain.

Source: [Miller’s Law — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/millers-law/).

## Apply in context

Use this lens when a design argument depends on how many items someone must hold in mind. Prefer meaningful chunks and visible information.

## Use judgment

The famous seven-plus-or-minus-two is not a menu, field, or navigation limit. The source itself warns against using it to impose arbitrary restrictions; capacity varies with people and context.

## Check the experience

Identify what must actually be recalled rather than recognized on screen. Test the task with interruptions instead of counting visible items.
