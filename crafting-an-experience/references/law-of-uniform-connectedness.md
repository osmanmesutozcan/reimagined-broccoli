# Law of Uniform Connectedness

## Core idea

A visible connection makes elements appear related.

Source: [Law of Uniform Connectedness — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/law-of-uniform-connectedness/).

## Apply in context

Use explicit connectors when a relationship must be traced. For example, connect a workflow condition to its resulting action and label the connector if the relationship needs explanation.

## Use judgment

A line can imply sequence, causation, or membership unintentionally. Dense connectors can obscure the very structure they should explain.

## Check the experience

Ask someone to trace a relationship and describe what it means. Confirm that the connection remains intelligible with realistic data density.
