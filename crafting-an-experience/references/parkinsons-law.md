# Parkinson’s Law

## Core idea

Work can expand to fill the time available; the site adapts this observation into a prompt to reduce unnecessary task duration.

Source: [Parkinson’s Law — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/parkinsons-law/).

## Apply in context

Give a task a clear finish and reduce repetitive work. For example, prefill known booking details while keeping them reviewable.

## Use judgment

This began as an observation about work, not a precise interface timing law. Artificial urgency can undermine thoughtful decisions; shortening work should preserve the time users need.

## Check the experience

Compare expected and actual effort. Identify repeated entry or optional work mistaken for requirements, and check whether the revised task still supports review.
