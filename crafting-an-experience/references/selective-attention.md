# Selective Attention

## Core idea

People attend to information related to their current goal and may miss other visible content or changes.

Source: [Selective Attention — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/selective-attention/).

## Apply in context

Place feedback near the focus of work and connect it to the action that caused it. For example, explain a failed save beside the relevant control or field.

## Use judgment

Greater visual intensity does not guarantee notice. Competing changes and banner-like styling can draw attention away or be ignored.

## Check the experience

Give someone a realistic task and observe whether they notice the relevant change unprompted. Verify that the feedback is available through their input and assistive tools.
