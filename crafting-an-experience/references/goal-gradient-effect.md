# Goal-Gradient Effect

## Core idea

People may increase effort as a meaningful goal feels nearer.

Source: [Goal-Gradient Effect — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/goal-gradient-effect/).

## Apply in context

Make real progress and remaining work understandable. For example, an application can identify completed sections and name the remaining requirements.

## Use judgment

Step counts can misrepresent unequal effort. Show earned progress and disclose newly discovered work; the goal should matter to the user.

## Check the experience

Ask someone what remains and how much effort they expect. Compare their answer with the actual remaining task and observe completion behavior.
