# Hick’s Law

## Core idea

Decision time tends to grow with the number and complexity of alternatives.

Source: [Hick’s Law — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/hicks-law/).

## Apply in context

Clarify labels, organize alternatives, and emphasize a relevant next action. For example, separate routine actions from infrequent configuration when they compete at a decision point.

## Use judgment

This is not a universal formula for navigation length or task duration. Familiarity, search, and clear categorization change the decision. Hiding actions can increase total effort.

## Check the experience

Time and observe the whole task, including finding hidden options. Does the change reduce hesitation without increasing wrong choices or backtracking?
