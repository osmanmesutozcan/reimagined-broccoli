# Serial Position Effect

## Core idea

Items at the beginning and end of a sequence may be recalled more readily than those in the middle.

Source: [Serial Position Effect — Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/serial-position-effect/).

## Apply in context

Consider placement when a sequence must be remembered. For example, open instructions with the goal and finish with the next action while keeping needed details available.

## Use judgment

Recall differs from findability. Preserve meaningful order, such as chronology or alphabetization, when that better supports retrieval.

## Check the experience

Ask what someone remembers after the sequence, then test finding a specific item. Check whether a recall improvement creates a search cost.
